Parallel agents need parallel checkouts. The usual answer, a fresh worktree per
task, pays for a cold `install` every time and leaves a trail of abandoned
folders. velmux gives each repo a small **pool of worktree slots** that are
claimed for a task and handed back when it's done, with `node_modules`, build
caches and all kept warm. Claiming one takes about a second.

## What you get

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Agents that manage their own worktrees" icon="rocket">
		Start a Claude in your repo; it plans there, claims a slot on its own branch
		when it's ready to change code, and hands it back when it's done.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Every agent at a glance" icon="list-format">
		One sidebar for every repo, slot and session, with each agent's state:
		working, blocked on you, finished. A notification and a sound when one
		needs you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Nothing lost" icon="approve-check">
		Hand a slot back mid-task and its work is parked, ready to resume, Claude
		conversation included. Quit, crash or reboot and every pane comes back,
		scrollback and all.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Slots that stay healthy" icon="random">
		Your checkout fast-forwards on its own; see which slots fell behind, sync
		them in one step, and give each its own ports.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Pull requests in the loop" icon="github">
		PR and CI status on every slot: open it, check it, and hand failing checks
		straight to the slot's Claude.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A proper multiplexer" icon="laptop">
		Tabs, splits, copy mode, mouse, themes, ⌘ shortcuts, conversation search,
		and live restarts that keep everything running.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
